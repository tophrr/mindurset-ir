### kartu-action
Use this card when starting feels harder than the task itself, or when the task has been postponed for several days. The two-minute version is not a trick to force a long session, so keep the stop rule honest and stop when the two minutes are over. If you stop and still feel stuck, write the next concrete step down before you close the document.
### kartu-all-or-nothing
Use this card when a sentence about yourself contains always, never, everything, or nothing, or when one result is being treated as a verdict on the whole person. Write the original sentence in full before you soften it, because the absolute word is easier to see when the sentence is on paper. Then count how many counter-examples the last week actually offers.
### kartu-rumination
Use this card when the same question has been replayed more than twice without producing a decision, especially late at night. Choose the ten-minute slot before the loop starts, not in the middle of it, because during the loop every other time feels wrong. If the worry is still sharp after the page is closed, write one sentence about what you will do tomorrow and leave it there.
### kartu-safety-plan
Use this card when thoughts of self-harm appear, when you notice you are isolating, or when someone tells you they are not safe. Read it before you need it, because a plan is hard to build in the moment it is needed. If you cannot reach anyone tonight, contact a crisis line and stay on the line while you decide the next step.
### kartu-self-compassion
Use this card when the inner voice is harsher than the voice you would use with a friend, or when shame is what stops you from asking for help. Keep the sentence accurate rather than kind for the sake of being kind, since an overgenerous sentence is easy to reject. One behavioural act done today is worth more than a paragraph of encouragement.
### kartu-sleep
Use this card when the night has become the hardest part of the day, when sleep has slipped later across several nights, or when the phone is the last thing you touch. Change one element at a time and give it several nights, because changing the routine and the bedtime together makes it impossible to tell what helped. If the pattern holds for a month, treat it as a health question rather than a discipline problem.
### kartu-stress
Use this card when the body is already signalling stress and the mind is treating one event as the whole story. Separate the event from the verdict out loud if writing is not possible, since the verdict is what turns a hard day into a judgement about yourself. Then shrink the next step until it fits the next ten minutes.
